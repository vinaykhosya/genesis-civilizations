\section*{Research Question Registry}

This registry documents the core scientific inquiries that Project Genesis intends to investigate. It links theoretical questions to the concrete laboratory instruments and experiments that provide the evidence.

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{RQ-001: Scarcity vs. Cooperation Dynamics}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbackslash{}textbf{Inquiry}: \textbackslash{}textit{Can stable cooperative attachment and sharing mechanisms emerge in environments constrained by high selection pressure?}
  \item \textbackslash{}textbf{Hypothesis}: If resource scarcity exceeds critical thresholds (scarcity \textgreater{} 4x), territorial disputes will rise, suppressing mutual attachment and reproduction rates, leading to early lineage extinction cascades.
  \item \textbackslash{}textbf{Supported Instruments}:
  \item \textbackslash{}texttt{OBS-001} (Population Dynamics)
  \item \textbackslash{}texttt{OBS-003} (Territorial Friction Map)
  \item \textbackslash{}texttt{OBS-004} (Social Kinship Grid)
  \item \textbackslash{}textbf{Experiments}:
  \item \textbackslash{}texttt{EXP-20260714-H4K9} (The Cooperation Paradox Under Scarcity)
\end{itemize}

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}

\subsection*{RQ-002: Genetic Entropy \& Selection Pressures}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbackslash{}textbf{Inquiry}: \textbackslash{}textit{Does high genetic mutation speed accelerate adaptability or trigger genetic collapse under volatile climate epochs?}
  \item \textbackslash{}textbf{Hypothesis}: Higher mutation rates (std \textgreater{} 0.08) accelerate early biome adaptation, but lead to rapid genetic drift and survival collapse when the climate enters a prolonged stable epoch.
  \item \textbackslash{}textbf{Supported Instruments}:
  \item \textbackslash{}texttt{OBS-001} (Population Dynamics)
